# BUYUNIC Enrollment Portal - Payment Instructions

## MTN Mobile Money Payment

### Method 1: USSD Code
1. Dial `*165*3#` on your MTN phone
2. Select "Pay Merchant" option
3. Enter Merchant ID: `693183`
4. Enter the amount to pay (as shown in your application)
5. Enter your MTN Mobile Money PIN
6. Confirm the transaction
7. Save the transaction reference/ID
8. Upload the payment screenshot or enter the reference in your application

### Method 2: MTN MoMo App
1. Open MTN MoMo App
2. Select "Pay Merchant"
3. Enter Merchant ID: `693183`
4. Enter the amount
5. Complete payment with your PIN
6. Save transaction reference
7. Upload proof of payment in your application

## After Payment
1. Save your transaction reference number
2. Take a screenshot of the payment confirmation
3. Log into your BUYUNIC enrollment portal
4. Go to "Payment" section
5. Upload your payment proof
6. Enter the transaction reference
7. Submit for verification

## Payment Verification
